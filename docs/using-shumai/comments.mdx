Shumai provides a review workspace designed for video, audio, and image feedback. Instead of generic text comments, you can drop timestamped feedback and sketch directly onto the media.

---

## Timestamped Comments

When reviewing video or audio files, comments can be anchored to specific seconds on the timeline:

1.  Play or scrub the video player to the exact frame or timestamp you want to review.
2.  Type your feedback in the comment box.
3.  Click **Submit**. Your comment will be saved with a timestamp marker shown on the video timeline.
4.  Collaborators can click your comment card in the sidebar to jump the video player to the exact moment you commented on.

## Draw Annotations (Canvas)

To give clear visual feedback, you can draw directly onto image or video frames:

1.  Pause the video on the frame you wish to annotate.
2.  Select a drawing tool (brush, rectangle, circle, arrow) from the annotation toolbar overlaying the player.
3.  Draw directly on the video screen.
4.  Type your comment and click **Submit**. Your drawing is saved along with your comment.
5.  When other users select your comment, your drawings will overlay on the player screen automatically at that frame.

## Comment Threads (Replies)

Keep discussions organized by replying directly to feedback:

- Click **Reply** on any comment.
- Replies are nested under the parent comment card, making it easy to track resolution conversations.

---

## Attachments

You can attach reference documents, style guides, or other assets to your comments:

- Click the **Paperclip** icon inside the comment input box.
- Choose a file to upload. It will appear as a downloadable link directly on your comment card.
