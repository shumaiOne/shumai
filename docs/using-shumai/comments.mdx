Shumai provides a feedback workflow designed for video, audio, and image review. Instead of generic text comments, you can drop timestamped feedback and sketch directly onto video frames or images.

---

## Time-Based Comments

When reviewing video or audio assets, comments can be anchored to specific moments in time:

1.  Play or scrub the video to the desired timestamp.
2.  Type your feedback in the comment input box.
3.  Click **Submit**. The comment is saved with the exact timestamp (`second` field in the database) and shown as a marker on the player timeline.
4.  Clicking a comment card in the comments sidebar automatically jumps the media player to that exact timestamp.

## Draw Annotations (Canvas)

To give precise visual feedback, you can draw directly onto image or video frames:

1.  Pause the video on the frame you wish to comment on.
2.  Select a drawing tool (brush, rectangle, circle, arrow) from the annotation toolbar overlay.
3.  Draw directly on the video player/canvas.
4.  Your drawing coordinates are captured and saved as a JSON payload (`annotation` field in `AssetComment`) associated with your comment.
5.  When other users click on your comment, the drawing is overlaid on the video player canvas at the corresponding frame.

## Comment Threads (Replies)

You can reply to any comment to create threaded discussions:

- Click **Reply** on any comment card.
- Type your message. Replies are nested under the parent comment using the `replyToId` field.
- Threaded comments are sorted chronologically.

---

## Comment Attachments

Need to attach a reference image, PDF, or document to your feedback?

- Click the **Paperclip** icon inside the comment input.
- Select the file to upload.
- Attachments are uploaded to the storage backend and linked to the comment via the `AssetCommentAttachment` relation.
