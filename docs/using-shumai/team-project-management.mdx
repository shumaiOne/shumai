Shumai uses a two-tier organizational hierarchy—**Teams** and **Projects**—to manage file access and collaboration.

---

## Teams vs. Projects

| Level       | Scope                                                                                                             | Administration             |
| :---------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------- |
| **Team**    | The parent organization boundary. Houses shared settings, integrations, AI providers, custom skills, and billing. | Managed by Team Owners.    |
| **Project** | A specific workspace containing folders, assets, comments, and collections.                                       | Managed by Project Owners. |

---

## Member Roles

Shumai uses permission roles to manage what actions team members can perform:

### Team Roles

- **Owner**: Full administrative control over team settings, custom skills, provider credentials, and sandbox restrictions.
- **Member**: General access to the team and its public projects.

### Project Roles

- **Owner**: Can configure project settings, manage invites, create collections, generate share links, and delete assets.
- **Editor**: Can upload files, delete their own assets, edit custom metadata values, and create comments or annotations.
- **Reviewer**: Read-only access to assets. Can view files, toggle versions, and leave text comments and drawings, but cannot edit metadata or delete files.

---

## Inviting Members

To add new members to a Team or Project:

1.  Navigate to the **Members** tab in your Team or Project settings.
2.  Click **Create Invite Link**.
3.  Select the invite role (Editor or Reviewer) and set the access duration.
4.  Copy the generated invite URL. When a recipient opens the link, they will be prompted to sign up or log in, after which they will automatically join the Team or Project.
