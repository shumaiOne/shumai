## Comments and Feedback

Shumai makes it easy to collaborate on assets through a robust commenting system.

### Adding Comments
1. Open any file or version stack.
2. Type your message in the comment panel on the right.
3. You can use **@mentions** to notify specific team members.
4. Press **Enter** to post.

### Replying to Comments
To keep conversations organized, you can reply directly to an existing comment to create a thread.

### AI Assistance in Comments
If an AI Agent is configured and mentioned in a comment, it can automatically process the asset (e.g., analyze an image or transcribe audio) and post a response directly in the thread.

## Team and Project Management

Manage who has access to your work at the team or project level.

### Team Members
Administrators can invite new members to the team. Members can be assigned different roles (e.g., Admin, Member).
- **Invite**: Use the **Invite Member** button in the Team Settings.
- **Roles**: Manage permissions by assigning roles.

### Project Members
You can also manage access on a per-project basis. This allows you to invite external collaborators or specific team members to a project without giving them access to the entire team.

## Notification Configuration

Stay informed about relevant activity without being overwhelmed.

### Personalizing Notifications
In your user settings, you can configure which actions trigger a notification:
- **Comments**: Get notified when someone comments on your files or mentions you.
- **Uploads**: Be alerted when new assets are added to projects you follow.
- **Workflow Completions**: Get notified when long-running tasks (like transcoding or AI analysis) are finished.

### Notification Channels
Shumai currently supports in-app notifications, with email support coming soon.
