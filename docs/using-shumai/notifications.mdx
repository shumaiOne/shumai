Shumai includes a built-in notification system to keep you informed of team activities, file statuses, and review comments. You can fully customize which events trigger notifications, check updates from your inbox, or toggle notifications for specific projects.

---

## Notification Settings

You can customize your notification preferences for each team to avoid unnecessary noise. To manage your notification events:

1. Click on **Team Settings** in the sidebar.
2. Select the **Notifications** tab.
3. Toggle the switches to enable or disable notifications for specific events:

### Comments & Replies

- **General Comments**: Notify me when someone comments on an asset I collaborate on.
- **Comment Replies**: Notify me when someone replies to my comment threads.
- **@Mentions**: Notify me when someone tags me (using `@username`) in any comment.

### Assets & Statuses

- **Your Uploads**: Notify me when files I upload finish transcoding and are ready for review.
- **Other Uploads**: Notify me when other team members upload new assets.
- **Status Updates**: Notify me when someone changes an asset's status (e.g., from "In Progress" to "Approved").

---

## Project-Level Notifications

If you want to mute a specific project entirely (except for direct `@mentions` which are always delivered):

1. Navigate to the **Project** dashboard.
2. Open **Project Settings**.
3. Toggle **Enable Notifications** on or off.

---

## The Notification Inbox

- The **Bell** icon in the navigation bar displays a red badge when you have unread notifications.
- Click the bell to open your notification feed panel.
- Click any notification card to mark it as read and jump directly to the relevant file and comment thread.
